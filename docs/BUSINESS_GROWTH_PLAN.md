# RamiroX Market — Business Growth Plan

## Principle
First ensure accurate, trustworthy orders; then increase qualified traffic; then optimize conversion. Traffic is not success if checkout, margins or fulfillment are unreliable.

## Funnel
1. Qualified landing sessions.
2. Category/product views.
3. Add-to-cart.
4. Checkout starts.
5. Valid order handoffs.
6. Confirmed and fulfilled orders.
7. Repeat customers/referrals.

Use existing free capabilities first. New analytics, advertising, paid APIs or recurring costs require explicit budget approval.

## Conversion foundations
- Clear photos and accurate product information.
- Consistent price display and transparent delivery/pickup costs.
- Clear contact, order and post-order expectations.
- Fast, accessible mobile experience.
- Helpful empty/error states; no misleading urgency.
- No fabricated reviews, scarcity, discounts or guarantees.

## Revenue discipline
Track gross sales separately from product cost, delivery, commissions, refunds, support and variable costs. Evaluate contribution margin before promotion or reinvestment. Never change stored prices or financial records during frontend work.

## First milestones
Protect order/commission integrity; validate the order journey with synthetic tests; improve mobile catalog and trust; establish organic discovery and conversion baselines; review actual results before paid acquisition.
